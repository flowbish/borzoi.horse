I've been getting into photography recently, and that has given me the urge to pull out my Game Boy Camera. I've had this thing for years and only slightly played around with it, mostly due to not having a good way to get photos off of it. The classic way is of course with a Game Boy Printer. I haven't had any luck finding one, mostly due to not looking so hard, but I realized recently that, duh, a rom cart reader would be able to extract the photos, and that's exactly what [GBxCart RW](https://shop.insidegadgets.com/product/gbxcart-rw/) and [FlashGBX](https://github.com/lesserkuma/FlashGBX) do.

{{ <gallery.embed manifest fragment="gbxcart" /> }}

It's super super easy to install and use, and gives an album view of the picture on the Game Boy Camera, as well as the ability to do a live view of the camera to give a very laggy webcam impression. It is super cool, and the photo export is very fast. I wish it could delete photos, because that's slow and annoying in the game, but maybe it's somewhere I missed, or maybe I should just delete the entire save state.

{{ <gallery.embed manifest fragment="flashgbx-album-view" /> }}

I think it's really cool, lo-fi way to capture life :3 It is really annoying because the camera does awful in low light, high light, high motion, and high contrast situations, but also, it rules, so whatever. I recommend it, 10/10. I'm thinking about what more I can do with this, including ways to use the live-view feature to assemble some kind of webcam, or at least video recording system. There's a lot to play with in that space.
