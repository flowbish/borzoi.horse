I love bike bags. I love bags dedicated to a specific purpose, and nothing has more specific purpose than a bag custom-fit to a bike's geometry. I also love bags that serve a specific, silly purpose, like holding [itemlabel dot com](https://itemlabel.com/) creatures.

{{ <gallery.embed manifest="/blog/oui-oui-frame-bag/gallery.toml" fragment="frame-bag-template-folded" /> }}

I made this bag following a process roughly like [this article from bikepacking.com](https://bikepacking.com/gear/how-to-make-a-bikepacking-framebag-krampus/). Create a template by tracing the inside of your bike frame (I left room for my water bottle). Trace that out onto your chosen fabric with seam allowance. Cut out a border piece roughly 2.25" wide (and long enough to make it all the way round the bag). Dress that up and sew it together and finish the seams. 

I made this out of clear vinyl so that you can see everything inside :3 I did a tape finish on the seams which is the only reasonable way to finish vinyl, plus it looks so legit. I added a really janky zipper to the side so I could fill it, and if I do this over again I'd definitely try to make that part look nicer. I'd also add some stiffener to the edge to give it a bit more structure.
